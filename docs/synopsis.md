Speakers read from the portrait beside each caption (original ROM, `tools/debug/pages.py rom/kitarou.sfc`).

## Plot
In 1999 Great King Enma's court sentences Kitaro to death. Yumeko Tendo, asked by the future Medama Oyaji, sends a
letter back in time with the Millstone (a time-travel millstone) to the Kitaro of the past. Past Kitaro, Medama
Oyaji and Shisa go to the Millstone; Kitaro sets it for June 1999, before the trial, but they arrive in July 1999,
after it. Nezumi Otoko tells them the date. The future Shisa has waited for them: Tantanbo wrecked that era's
Millstone as he died, so they cannot jump back. They ride the Train of the Dead to the execution ground to save
"the Kitaro of this era". The train crashes; Jigoku Doji wakes Kitaro, and a witch steals the Orb that Great King
Enma gave Jigoku Doji. Nurarihyon gloats that the execution is long over; he called the space yokai (names in an
invented alien script), who mean to revive their Great King (Great King Tenma), sealed long ago by Earth's yokai.
The Orb grants four wishes, one of which revives him. After the final battle the Orb has three wishes left: restore
the town, revive this era's Kitaro, repair the Millstone so past Kitaro can go home. Jigoku Doji stays to fight a
foe who won't die. Back in his own time Kitaro thanks Yumeko, who has not sent the letter yet ("Huh?"), and he
covers it up.
